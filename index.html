01 / About

A creative mind with many voices.

My creative journey is rooted in curiosity — the curiosity to understand people, emotions and the stories hidden within everyday moments.

From putting feelings into poetry and developing stories for books to discovering expression through singing and art, I’m constantly experimenting with new ways to communicate. This portfolio is a growing collection of ideas, experiments and creative projects.

I believe good creative work doesn't simply communicate an idea. It creates a feeling that stays with you after the work is finished.
